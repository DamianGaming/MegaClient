## MegaClient 1.9.7 Update Log

- Updated the built-in MegaClient client to **version 0.14.2** for **Minecraft 26.2**.
- Improved launcher performance while Minecraft is running by automatically reducing non-essential visual effects and background interface activity.
- Improved performance when the launcher is minimised, hidden or not focused.
- Redesigned the home status area with clearer selected-instance, protection and performance information.
- Improved launch and title-bar status indicators so it is easier to see what MegaClient is currently doing.
- Reduced live console resource usage while keeping useful recent launch information available.
- Improved loading of mod, resource-pack, server and account images.
- Fixed a cosmetics-related launcher issue that could affect cape actions.
- Included additional stability, responsiveness and visual consistency improvements.
